Mega desk

Before you startMega desk

Two people, about 40 minutes. Build it where it will stand: the finished desk does not fit through a door. Legs and rails are 3030 extrusion; every joint is a corner bracket with T-nuts, so leave all screws finger-tight until the top is on, then square it up and tighten.
